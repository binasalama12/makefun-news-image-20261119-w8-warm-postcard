One image, the whole of Thursday 19 November 2026: 31 verified stories drawn and typeset as a single front page. 3 hero panels lead — the first Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral in Shenzhen, GTA VI's worldwide launch, and Anthropic's record IPO — above 10 pictured panels, a dot-matrix world map whose numbered pins match the stories, and 18 numbered briefs from Washington to Wellington.

A warm postcard edition, craft first: three big hero panels, and every pictured story stamped '19 NOV' like a postcard from its dateline, above a dot-matrix world map and the numbered briefs.

Every story is drawn from the day's reporting (BBC, Reuters, AP, Al Jazeera, Le Monde, CBS, Japan Times wires); nothing is invented and no live clock times are claimed. `main.py` validates the image, prints the 31-story index, and copies `entry.png` to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
